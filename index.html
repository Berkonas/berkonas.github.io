01 About

My work is driven by a simple goal: designing systems that are affordable, accessible, and genuinely useful for real people.

I’m a mechanical engineer and researcher building human-centered technology at the intersection of robotics, haptics, and healthcare. I combine mechanical and biomedical engineering to help people, whether that means an engineering solution, policy writing, or volunteering.

Beyond titles and degrees, I’m motivated by curiosity and hands-on problem solving. Whether I’m prototyping a new haptic device, digging into interdisciplinary research, or experimenting in the kitchen, I learn by doing. This site is a snapshot of my work and the interests that shape how I think and build.

03 What I work on

Machines built around people.

01

Haptics

Interfaces that turn information into touch, from a vibrotactile navigation belt to studies of how people match intensity across senses.

02

Robotics

Kinematics, dynamics, and control for manipulators and human-in-the-loop systems, like resolved-rate and IK control on a UR5.

03

Rehab & wearables

Rehabilitation engineering, prosthetics, and wearable devices that restore and extend what bodies can do.

04

Healthcare & policy

Medical devices, telemedicine, and the science and technology policy that gets them to the people who need them.

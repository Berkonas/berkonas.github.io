Conventions and method

Frames. Rotations are active and right-handed. A matrix R maps vectors in the rotated frame to the world frame, so its columns are the rotated axes. Quaternions follow the Hamilton convention, q = w + xi + yj + zk, and q and −q are the same rotation.

Euler angles. Intrinsic Z-Y-X means rotate about Z, then about the new Y′, then about the new X″: R = Rz(α) Ry(β) Rx(γ). Extrinsic X-Y-Z rotates about the fixed axes and produces the same matrix. At gimbal lock the outer angles are not unique; one of them is set to zero.

Matrices you type are checked for orthonormality. If one is slightly off, as measured or rounded data often is, it is snapped to the nearest rotation by polar decomposition. A reflection (det < 0) is rejected.

Limb rows are for one side. Moments of inertia are about each segment’s own center of mass. The three trunk parts sum to the whole trunk.

Data, endpoints and method

Source. de Leva P. (1996). Adjustments to Zatsiorsky–Seluyanov’s segment inertia parameters. Journal of Biomechanics 29(9): 1223–1230, Table 4. Reference subjects: 100 men (1.741 m, 73.0 kg) and 15 women (1.735 m, 61.9 kg), young adults.

Scaling. Masses are the published fractions of body mass. Lengths are the reference lengths scaled by stature. Centers of mass and radii of gyration are fractions of each length, so a measured length changes them too: I = m (r · L)².

Endpoints. VERT vertex · MIDG mid-gonion · SUPR suprasternale · XYPH xiphoid · OMPH omphalion · MIDH midpoint of the hip joint centers · SJC, EJC, WJC, HJC, KJC shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee joint centers · MET3 third metacarpale · LMAL lateral malleolus · HEEL · TTIP tip of the longest toe. Centers of mass are measured from the first endpoint listed.

Actuators and perception

LRAs only vibrate strongly near their resonance, commonly 150–235 Hz, so the carrier is locked to it and the envelope does the work. ERMs tie frequency to amplitude and need tens of milliseconds to spin up, so very short pulses feel soft. Voice-coil tactors follow any waveform and are usually driven by an audio amplifier, which is what the WAV export is for.

Skin. The Pacinian corpuscles that sense vibration are most sensitive around 200–300 Hz; below about 50 Hz the sensation shifts to flutter. Laptop speakers reproduce little under 150 Hz, so preview low carriers on headphones.

Arduino export writes one PWM duty value every 5 ms. Drive the motor through a transistor or a driver IC, never straight from the pin.
